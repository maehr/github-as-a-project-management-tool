## D-000 Title of the decision

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** proposed | accepted | replaced by D-000
- **Context:** What happened, and why a decision is necessary.
- **Decision:** What you decided. One action per line.
- **Consequences:** What follows from the decision for scope, dates, data, or access.
- **Not changed:** What the decision explicitly does not change, for example an official deadline.
- **Source:** The rule, the message, or the meeting that supports the decision.
- **Links:** The Issues and pull requests that implement the decision.
