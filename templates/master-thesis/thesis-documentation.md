## 1. Question

What does the thesis ask? State one main research question and, if needed, up to three sub-questions.

## 2. Scope

What does the thesis include, and what does it exclude? Use a table with two columns: "In scope" and "Out of scope".

## 3. Provenance

Where does each source or dataset come from? Name the holder, the date of access, the version, and the identifier. State which material is your own and which belongs to others.

## 4. Method

How do you analyse the material? Name the steps, the tools with their versions, and the parameters. Link the scripts in `src/` or `analysis/`.
